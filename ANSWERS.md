# SprintQuest — Master Questionnaire Answers

## Phase 1: Problem & User (Domains 1-5)

### Domain 1: Vision & Purpose
**Q1.1 Product in one sentence:**
A platform that turns Sprint Reviews and Retrospectives into a gamified experience with XP, quests, badges, and team progression — making Continuous Improvement feel like a game, not a chore.

**Q1.2 Problem:**
Teams treat retrospectives and sprint reviews as boring, unproductive rituals. Research shows 44% of action items are never completed, only 35% of teams consistently complete retro actions, stakeholders often don't attend sprint reviews, and teams revisit the same problems sprint after sprint.

**Q1.3 Why it matters:**
86% of software development uses Agile. Teams spend ~2 hours/sprint on retros. When ceremonies produce no follow-through, teams lose engagement and Agile becomes pure overhead.

**Q1.4 3-year vision:**
Become the platform teams use to run every ceremony — from planning to retro — with the entire Agile cycle gamified as a persistent team journey.

**Q1.5 What this is NOT:**
Not a retro board, not a productivity tracker, not a project management tool, not an individual leaderboard.

**Q1.6 "It worked" looks like:**
Teams look forward to retros, action items completed at >80% rate (vs current ~40%), stakeholders actively participate, teams show measurable improvement over consecutive sprints.

**Q1.7 Principles we won't sacrifice:**
Team XP only (no individual leaderboards), privacy-first, low-friction onboarding, AI as facilitator not decision-maker, sits on top of existing tools.

### Domain 2: Problem
**Q2.1 What exactly is the problem?**
Multiple verified overlapping problems:
- Action items die: 44% never completed (ResearchGate 2025), only 35% consistently completed (Scrum Alliance 2023)
- Retros become checkbox exercises: same formats sprint after sprint (Kaizenko 2025)
- Stakeholders don't attend sprint reviews (Scrum.org documented challenge)
- No objective data in retros: teams collect data but seldom use it (arXiv 2025, 19 practitioners)
- Psychological safety issues: data access is role-specific
- Same problems recur without resolution

**Q2.2 Evidence:**
VERIFIED: 44% action item completion, 40-50% retro completion (Easy Agile), stakeholder disengagement (Scrum.org), only ~19% use objective data in retros (arXiv 2025).

**Q2.3 Frequency:**
Every sprint (2 weeks). Every ceremony, every team, every sprint.

**Q2.4 Who is affected?**
Scrum Masters, Agile Coaches, Product Owners, Developers, QA, Stakeholders, Engineering Managers.

**Q2.5 Cost:**
VERIFIED: Top 20% engaged teams have 41% less absenteeism, 59% less turnover (Gallup). Poor retros = disengaged teams = turnover.

**Q2.6 If unsolved:**
Teams abandon Agile ceremonies as rituals. Retros become "zombie meetings."

**Q2.7 Big enough?**
VERIFIED: Enterprise Agile transformation market: $41.2B (2024) → $48.75B (2025) → $96.28B by 2029 (CAGR 18.5%). Agile tools market: $5.7B (2020) → $9.2B (2024).

Understand what was built, give meaningful feedback, see progress, feel involved.

**Q4.9 What makes them switch:**
If current tools don't produce measurable improvement in 2-3 sprints. If a tool makes retros actually fun and productive.

## Phase 2: Value Proposition (Domain 6)

**Q6.1 Why use SprintQuest?**
It makes the boring part of Agile (ceremonies) engaging and productive. Teams look forward to retros and sprint reviews.

**Q6.2 Primary promise:**
"Convert your Agile ceremonies from rituals into missions. Every sprint is a season. Every team levels up."

**Q6.3 Result:**
Higher action completion (target >80%), better stakeholder participation, measurable improvement over time, teams look forward to ceremonies.

**Q6.4 Time saved?**
Not necessarily time — makes time spent more productive. 60 minutes of focused, gamified improvement vs 60 minutes of unproductive retro.

**Q6.5 Emotion:**
Excitement, anticipation, team pride, achievement. Not anxiety or surveillance.

**Q6.6 What makes us different?**
No existing tool gamifies the FULL Scrum cycle as a game. EasyRetro/GoRetro/TeamRetro make retros more interactive but not game-like. SprintQuest turns each sprint into a persistent mission with XP, quests, level progression across sprints.

**Q6.7 "Aha moment":**
Team completes first sprint, earns Team XP, unlocks a badge, sees next quest — realizes this is NOT another retro board.

**Q6.8 10 seconds:**
"It's Kahoot for your Agile ceremonies — teams earn XP, complete quests, and level up every sprint."

**Q6.9 30 seconds:**

**Q6.10 What NOT to promise:**
"Will fix your team's problems." We can only make ceremonies more engaging and provide structure for improvement. Improvement still comes from the team.

**Q7.12 Avatars:** Yes. Team mascots (🐉🦊🚀🤖🐙).

**Q7.13 Mascots:** Yes. Predefined team mascots.

**Q7.14 Collectibles:** Yes. Badges, themes, unlockable content.

**Q7.15 Rewards:** Unlock new game modes, avatars, themes, special challenges.

**Q7.16 Unlockables:** New quests, challenges, features at certain levels.

**Q7.17 Narrative:** Yes. Each sprint is a chapter. "Season 1: The Pipeline Rescue."

**Q7.18 Seasons:** Yes. Quarterly/yearly seasons with special events.

**Q7.19 Special events:** Yes. Seasonal challenges, surprise missions, limited-time events.

**Q7.20 Competition:** Team vs team within platform. NOT individual.

**Q7.21 Cooperation:** Yes, primary. Team XP is collaborative.

**Q7.22 Team vs team:** Yes, optional Team Battle mode.

**Q7.23 Individual competition:** No. Explicitly avoided.

**Q7.24 Avoid individual rankings:** Yes, absolutely.

**Q7.25 Avoid toxic behavior:** NO individual metrics. Team XP only. Scrum Master can disable competition. Anonymous participation. No visible individual contribution metrics.

**Q7.26 Incentivize:** Team collaboration, completing actions, participating, identifying recurring problems, stakeholder engagement, continuous improvement, admitting mistakes safely.

**Q7.27 NOT incentivize:** Excessive comments for XP, individual productivity, volume over quality, gaming the system, unnecessary ceremonies, competitive individual behavior.

**Q7.28 XP calculation:**
- Participating in ceremony: +10 XP
- Completing retro action: +50-200 XP (by difficulty)
- Sprint Review challenge: +100 XP
- Identifying recurring problem: +30 XP
- Completing a quest: +300 XP
- Team streak bonus: +50 XP per consecutive sprint with completed actions
- Stakeholder participation: +20 XP per vote/comment

**Q7.29 Who receives XP:** Team collectively. Not individuals.

**Q7.30 When:** End of each ceremony/sprint, distributed automatically.

**Q7.31 Lose XP?** No. Cumulative. Streaks can break.

**Q7.32 Levels:** Yes. Level 1 to 50+.

**Q7.33 Level up by:** Accumulating Team XP. Each level requires more XP.

**Q7.34 Level up unlocks:** New game modes, themes, avatars, badge types, special challenges.

**Q7.35 Achievement unlocks:** Completing specific milestones (first retro, 5 actions, first quest, 10-sprint streak, etc.).

**Q7.36 Value:** Primarily symbolic/badge-of-honor. Could unlock premium features at higher tiers.

**Q7.37 Personalization:** Yes. Teams choose game modes, set XP rules (within guardrails), choose difficulty.

**Q7.38 Scrum Master disable:** Yes. Full control to disable gamification.

**Q7.39 Choose modes:** Yes. Boss Battle, Sailboat, Mission Control, Detective, Team Battle.

## Phase 4: Psychology & Ethics (Domain 8)

**Q8.1 Behaviors to reinforce:**
Team collaboration, continuous improvement, psychological safety, action completion, transparent communication, proactive problem-solving.

**Q8.2 Why:**
Directly improve product quality, team morale, project outcomes. Gallup: top 20% engaged teams = 41% less absenteeism, 59% less turnover.

**Q8.3 Side effects of incentives:**
Individual XP → gaming system. Streaks → anxiety. Visible team XP → pressure. Badges → vanity metrics over substance.

**Q8.4 Farm XP?**
Yes possible. Mitigation: cap XP per action type, verify through Jira integration, require meaningful participation (not just volume).

**Q8.5 Feel forced to participate?**
Yes possible. Mitigation: anonymous options, Scrum Master controls visibility, opt-out for certain activities.

**Q8.6 Social pressure?**
Yes at team level. Mitigation: XP is team-level, not individual, streaks are private to team.

**Q8.7 Embarrassment?**
If team "loses." Mitigation: no "losing" mechanic, XP is cumulative, focus on improvement not comparison.

**Q8.8 Negative competition?**
Between teams in Team Battle. Mitigation: opt-in only, no permanent rankings, focus on collaborative achievements.

**Q8.9 Protect introverts?**
Anonymous participation, written input (not forced to speak), small-group modes, no public individual metrics.

**Q8.10 Protect anonymity?**
Anonymous voting during retros, pseudonymous comments option, facilitator controls who sees what.

**Q8.11 Manager use data against employee?**
NO. Team-level data only by default. Manager access requires explicit team consent. No individual attribution without team approval.

**Q8.12 Private information:**
Individual contributions (unless team opts in), retro discussion content, voting data (anonymous by default), action item details.

**Q8.13 Manager can see:**
Team-level aggregates only: XP, streaks, badges, ceremonies completed, action completion rate. NO individual metrics.

**Q8.14 Team-exclusive information:**
All retro discussion content, individual contributions, anonymous votes, action item details.

**Q8.15 Ban individual metrics:**
Yes, explicitly. No individual productivity metrics.

**Q8.16 Avoid surveillance:**
No keystroke logging, no screen monitoring, no individual activity tracking, no productivity scores. Only ceremony-level engagement metrics.

**Q9.4 AI can suggest dynamics:**
Yes. Based on team performance history, recurring issues, engagement patterns.

**Q9.5 Adapt to team:**
Yes. Introverted teams → more written input. Experienced teams → more complex modes. Struggling teams → simpler, encouraging formats.

**Q9.6 How many rounds:**
4-5 rounds: Icebreaker → Data Gathering → Detection → Decision → Quest/Action.

**Q9.7 Timer:** Yes. 60-90 seconds per person for icebreakers/data gathering.

**Q9.8 Music:** Optional ambient background. Not MVP.

**Q9.9 Animations:** Yes, light animations for results reveal, badge unlocks, XP gained.

**Q9.10 Random events:** Yes. Surprise questions, wild cards, special challenges.

**Q9.11 Dynamic questions:** Yes. AI generates based on team context and history.

**Q9.12 Voting:** Yes. Dot voting, ranking, weighted voting.

**Q9.13 Anonymous voting:** Yes, optional.

**Q9.14 Votes per person:** Configurable, default: 3 per person per category.

**Q9.15 Change votes:** Yes, until voting round closes.

**Q9.16 Auto-group ideas:** Yes. AI groups similar ideas. Team can manually adjust.

**Q9.17 Detect recurring themes:** Yes. AI identifies recurring themes across sprints.

**Q9.18 Prioritize:** Yes. Voting determines priority. AI flags high-impact items.

**Q9.19 Convert to actions:** Yes. Selected items become quests with owners and deadlines.

**Q9.20 Assign owners:** Yes. Each action/quest needs a named owner.

**Q9.21 Set dates:** Yes. Due dates for each action.

**Q9.22 Track:** Yes. Actions tracked across sprints, visible in next retro.

**Q9.23 Uncompleted actions:** Carried over to next retro with explanation. After 3 sprints: flagged as stalled.

**Q9.24 Next retro:** Previous actions reviewed first (5 min). Status shown. Uncompleted items discussed.

## Phase 18: North Star Metric (Domain 22)

**Q22.1 North Star Metric:**
**Number of improvement actions completed by active teams per month.**

**Q22.2 Why this metric?**
Directly measures whether the product drives continuous improvement — the core value proposition. Captures engagement, completion, and actual impact.

**Q22.3 What behavior shows value?**
Teams are not just having ceremonies — they are completing actions that improve their work. Difference between "ceremony tool" and "continuous improvement platform."

**Q22.4 What metric would be deceptive?**
Number of ceremonies started (doesn't measure quality), total XP earned (could be gamed), number of users registered (doesn't measure engagement), number of badges earned (vanity).

**Q22.5 What to optimize?**
Action completion rate per team per sprint. Primary metric.

**Q22.6 What NOT to use as proxy?**
Number of comments (volume ≠ quality), session duration (longer ≠ better), login frequency (presence ≠ engagement).

**Q22.7 Primary metric to optimize:**
Team XP earned from completed actions. Combines participation, completion, and quality into one measurable outcome.
